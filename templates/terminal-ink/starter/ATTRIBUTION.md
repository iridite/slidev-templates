# Attribution and provenance

## Repository-owned work

The `terminal-ink` starter, its example content, CSS, documentation, and SVG preview are original clean-room work maintained by the `slidev-templates` project and licensed under MIT.

## Research boundary

Developer-oriented Slidev starters and operational presentation workflows were surveyed, including multi-talk and tested-template projects. No upstream source code, logos, screenshots, or slide content are included.

Public repositories were used to understand presentation jobs and ecosystem gaps, not as source material to copy. No third-party deck code, institution or company branding, screenshots, fonts, logos, or presentation assets are bundled.

## Runtime dependencies

The starter depends on the open-source Slidev application and default theme packages. Their code is installed from npm and remains under its own upstream license; it is not vendored into this template.

- Slidev: https://github.com/slidevjs/slidev — MIT
- Slidev themes: https://github.com/slidevjs/themes — MIT
- Vue: https://github.com/vuejs/core — MIT

Dependency notices and licenses are distributed by their respective packages. The template's own MIT license is in [`LICENSE`](./LICENSE).
